# 📊 HR Analytics Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

This HR Analytics Dashboard provides comprehensive insights into workforce demographics, employee attrition, income distribution, and departmental performance.

The dashboard is designed to help HR teams and decision-makers identify workforce trends, monitor employee retention, and support strategic planning.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze employee attrition patterns
- Understand workforce demographics
- Monitor departmental performance
- Evaluate employee income distribution
- Identify high-risk job roles
- Improve data-driven HR decision making

---

## 🛠 Tools & Technologies

- Power BI Desktop
- Power Query
- DAX
- Data Modeling

---

## 📊 Dashboard Pages

### 1️⃣ HR Overview Dashboard

#### KPIs
- Total Employees
- Total Attritions
- Active Employees
- Attrition Rate %
- Overtime Attrition %

#### Visuals
- Monthly Attrition Trend
- Attrition by Job Role
- Employees by Department
- Attrition Rate by Department

---

### 2️⃣ Income Analysis Dashboard

#### KPIs
- Total Income
- Average Income
- Active Employees
- Total Attritions

#### Visuals
- Total Income by Month
- Total Income by Department
- Average Income by Job Role
- Age Group Distribution
- Attrition by Marital Status

---

## 🔍 Key Insights

- Attrition Rate: 16.53%
- Overtime employees show significantly higher attrition.
- Sales Department has the highest attrition rate.
- Research & Development contains the largest workforce.
- Healthcare Representatives have the highest average income.
